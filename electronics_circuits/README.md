# Electronics and circuits

Circuit, device and electronic system models.

Each data set below has a short summary of the science: what the model computes, what varies, and what the active subspace shows. The numbers come from the data set's notebook, which has the details and the comparison with the paper. The [top-level README](../README.md#data-sets) has the paper links and the kind of each data set (stored data or Python model).

### [Cavity_Shielding_Effectiveness](Cavity_Shielding_Effectiveness/)

The shielding effectiveness (SE) of a cavity is the ratio of the field inside it to the incident field. Campione et al. rank six parameters of a slotted aluminium cylinder (wall thickness d, slot length ℓ_p, slot width w, cavity height h and radius a, and wall conductivity σ) by Sobol' indices of a matched power-balance bound on its SE between 1 and 3 GHz, and find that the slot width and slot length carry almost all of the variance. The notebook implements that closed-form bound in numpy with complex-step gradients, reproduces the paper's one-at-a-time sweeps and Sobol' indices, and estimates the active subspace of the bound at 1, 2 and 3 GHz and of its 1–3 GHz mean from 1000 Monte Carlo samples. Each output has a dominant direction set by the slot width (λ₁/λ₂ = 43.1 at 2 GHz), almost the same at every frequency, and a clear second direction, mostly the slot length, which at 1 GHz is better separated than the first (λ₂/λ₃ = 55.0 against λ₁/λ₂ = 34.4), so the notebook takes two dimensions there and one elsewhere. A cubic in the first active variable has leave-one-out R² 0.97182 at 2 GHz.

### [Two_Stage_CMOS_Op_Amp](Two_Stage_CMOS_Op_Amp/)

Settaluri et al. train a reinforcement-learning agent (AutoCkt) to size a two-stage CMOS operational amplifier in a 45 nm predictive technology. The notebook simulates the same circuit with ngspice over the paper's design box, six transistor multipliers and a compensation capacitor, each on a log scale, and computes active subspaces of four specifications from finite-difference gradients: the gain and supply current over 1000 designs of the whole box, and the unity-gain bandwidth and phase margin over 1000 designs that meet the paper's lower gain target of 200 V/V. The supply current (λ₁/λ₂ = 8.8) and the bandwidth (λ₁/λ₂ = 19.1) each have one dominant direction, a ratio of device sizes that hand analysis predicts: the tail-to-reference mirror ratio for the current, and tail current over compensation capacitance for the bandwidth; a cubic in one active variable has leave-one-out R² 0.894 and 0.949. The gain has a dominant direction, the second-stage current balance of the hand analysis (|cos| = 0.993), inside a three-dimensional active subspace (λ₃/λ₄ = 16.5), and radial basis functions in three active variables reach leave-one-out R² 0.940. Phase margin needs two directions (λ₂/λ₃ = 4.0; leave-one-out R² 0.957 with two against 0.582 with one), the second almost the compensation capacitor alone, and these directions hold only for high-gain designs.
